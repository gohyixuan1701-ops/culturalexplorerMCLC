## What is still missing

- Total respondent count and the response denominator for each question.
- Exact question wording, answer options, skip logic and whether additions were single/multiple choice.
- Raw counts and confirmation that displayed percentages/scores match the latest responses.
- Collection dates, recruitment method, duplicate handling and relevant research-user roles.
- Whether all respondents used the same prototype; the existing revised deck describes post-use feedback mainly from Chinese Studies participants, but this was not independently verified here.

Do not infer a sample of eight from 12.5% increments; several sample sizes can produce those percentages. Age does not identify undergraduate, lecturer or postgraduate status. Do not infer actual conversion, representativeness, paid adoption or causation from these charts.

## Product implications to test

1. Prioritise permission-cleared excerpts and document depth for research users.
2. Define useful free-level material and a full paid content offer.
3. Treat RM30–60/year as a student price band to investigate, not an approved membership price.
4. Investigate why respondents are unsure or unlikely to subscribe.
5. Test repeat research use and paid demand across undergraduates, lecturers and postgraduates.

The pitch must cite these as existing-chart feedback until the Form data is reconciled. No current-Form freshness claim should appear in the slides.
