## Speaker guidance

- Demonstrate what works now before describing the planned membership service.
- Say “all content offered by the platform” for the paid tier. Do not promise full books, unrestricted downloads or third-party databases that are not available.
- Do not say the prototype is already a working subscription service.
- Call the chart figures exploratory existing-slide feedback. Do not state a respondent count or roles that have not been verified.
- A price preference is not a purchase; “probably subscribe” is not an achieved conversion rate.
- Explain how useful research material will differ from the free preview, then test that difference with actual users.
- Do not claim a school addressable market, school licences, classroom pilots, or sponsorship income as this model.
- Do not claim academic effectiveness, automatic preservation of full texts, institution endorsement or competitor gaps without evidence.
